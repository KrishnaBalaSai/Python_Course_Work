Why do we switch to OOP?
We switch to Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) 
when programs become large, complex, and difficult to maintain using procedural programming.

Main reasons:-
To manage large programs
Code reusability
Easy maintenance
Data security
Real-world representation
Easy teamwork
Flexibility
Hides unnecessary complexity
